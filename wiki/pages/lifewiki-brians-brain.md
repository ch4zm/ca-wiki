## Summary

Describes [[brians-brain](pages/brians-brain.md)]: its definition as Seeds with an extra
refractory state, its neural reading, and its patterns.[^1]
- Soups explode at lightspeed; the c/4 diagonal butterfly is common.
- Every pattern is a phoenix, so stable patterns are hard; no period-2 oscillators exist,
  and period-3 and period-4 oscillators are known.
- Photon lasers support reflectors, a duplicator, and AND, OR and XOR gates.
- A Rule 110 unit cell (Yoel Matveyev, 2020) makes the rule Turing-complete.
