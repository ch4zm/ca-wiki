## Summary

Describes [[seeds-rule](pages/seeds-rule.md)]: definition, rule integer and black/white
reversal, origin (Silverman, named by Wójtowicz), a Rule 110 "unit stripe" proof of
Turing-completeness (Peter Naszvadi, 2020), and its patterns.[^1]
- No still lifes; oscillators of many periods, the smallest being the 2-cell duoplet.
- Four-cell lightspeed photons, plus slower orthogonal and diagonal spaceships.
- Period-4 photon guns too dense for signal logic; puffers, rakes and a rake puffer breeder.
