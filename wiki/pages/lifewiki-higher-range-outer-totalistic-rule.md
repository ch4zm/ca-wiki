## Summary

Describes [[higher-range-outer-totalistic-rule](pages/higher-range-outer-totalistic-rule.md)].[^1]
- HROT generalizes both Larger than Life (any count lists, not one interval) and range-1
  outer-totalistic rules; Generations-style decay is allowed.
- Supported by Golly from 4.0 and by LifeViewer.
- Notation Rr,Cc,Slist,Blist,Nn: range 1-500, 0-256 states, and a neighbourhood code
  from Moore and von Neumann through circular, checkerboard, hash, cross, saltire, star,
  hexagonal, tripod, triangular, custom and weighted.
