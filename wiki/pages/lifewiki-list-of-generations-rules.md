**Date ingested:** 2026-09-25

## Summary

A table of about fifty named [[generations-rule](pages/generations-rule.md)]s with their
rulestrings in both notations, a character label, author, date and description. Many
descriptions come from MCell and quote rule authors such as John Elliott.[^1] Entries
used in this wiki include [[brians-brain](pages/brians-brain.md)], [[star-wars-rule](pages/star-wars-rule.md)], Bombers, Brain 6,
Faders, Lava, Lines and RainZha.[^1]
