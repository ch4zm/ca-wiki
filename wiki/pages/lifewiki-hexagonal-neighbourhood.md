**Type:** wiki article

## Summary

Describes [[hexagonal-neighbourhood](pages/hexagonal-neighbourhood.md)].[^1]
- The cells adjacent to a region on the hexagonal tiling, notated with an H suffix
  (B2/S34H).
- Range n holds 3n(n + 1) cells; the asterisk (six rays) and tripod (three of the six
  neighbours, intrinsically chiral) are alternative extensions.
- Isotropic hex rules use Paul Callahan's o/m/p (ortho/meta/para) letters, borrowed from
  aromatic chemistry.
- The hex grid has its own symmetry set (C3, C6, D6, D12 and more) for soup searches.
- Golly, LifeViewer, apgsearch and Catagolue each support part of this rulespace.
