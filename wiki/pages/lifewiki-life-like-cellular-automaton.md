## Summary

Defines the family ([[life-like-cellular-automaton](pages/life-like-cellular-automaton.md)])
and maps its rule space.[^1]
- Four criteria: square grid, two states, Moore neighbourhood, outer-totalistic rule.
- 262,144 rules, each paired with a complement that behaves the same with on and off
  swapped; 512 rules are their own complement.
- The lowest birth count largely fixes a rule's character: B1 grows at lightspeed, B2
  explodes, B4 and up is stable, so chaotic rules must have B3 or B0 without S8.
- Generalizations: isotropic non-totalistic, non-isotropic, Generations and Larger than
  Life rules.
