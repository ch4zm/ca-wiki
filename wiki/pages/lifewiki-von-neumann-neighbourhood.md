## Summary

Describes [[von-neumann-neighbourhood](pages/von-neumann-neighbourhood.md)].[^1]
- The cells orthogonally adjacent to a region; for one cell, its four edge neighbours,
  that is, the cells at Manhattan distance 1. Whether the centre counts depends on context.
- 2n cells in n dimensions (6 in 3D, an octahedron); range n by iterating, 2n(n + 1) cells.
- Two-state rules on it cannot move patterns without B1, which makes everything grow at
  lightspeed, so spaceships are impossible; a three-state rule has many.
- Banks-I is an early two-state rule on it.
