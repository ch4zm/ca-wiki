## Summary

A short survey of how rules are written as strings ([[rulestring](pages/rulestring.md)]).[^1]
- Birth/survival notation B{counts}/S{counts} is the standard: Life is B3/S23, Seeds B2/S.
- The survival-first S/B form (Life = 23/3) is older and falling out of use.
- A trailing V means the von Neumann neighbourhood, a trailing H the hexagonal one.
- Hensel notation adds neighbour arrangements to the counts for non-totalistic rules;
  Generations rules add a state count; Larger than Life rules have their own form.
- Rule integers and base-64 MAP strings are the non-string and fully general
  alternatives.
