# Beehive

## Description

The beehive is a six-cell [[still-life](pages/still-life.md)]. A straight line of four
live cells becomes one in two generations, passing through a 3 × 2 rectangle sometimes
called the *pre-beehive*.[^1] Beehives are frequently produced and are among the
commonest objects in random ash.[^2]

**Honey farm.** Four beehives form the honey farm, stable as a group
([[familiar-fours](pages/familiar-fours.md)]). A seven-cell pre-honey farm becomes one in
17 generations, and a horizontal row of seven cells becomes one in 14.[^3]

**Made and eaten.** The [[queen-bee](pages/queen-bee.md)] leaves a beehive every 15
generations. A [[block](pages/block.md)] next to a beehive destroys it in 7 generations,
which is how the queen bee shuttle is stabilized.[^4]
