# Beehive

## Description

The beehive is a six-cell [[still-life](pages/still-life.md)]. Two of the tetrominoes
become beehives on the second move and a third on the third; Gardner calls beehives
"frequently produced patterns".[^1] Wolfram's survey calls it the "hexagon" of six live
cells.[^2]

**Honey farm.** Four beehives together make the *honey farm*, a stable group. A horizontal
row of seven counters becomes a honey farm after 14 moves. A five-by-five block produces
the fourth generation of that history in one move, so it becomes a honey farm after 11
moves.[^3] A row of 12 counters ends with two beehives, and a row of four 5-cell segments
ends with four honey farms and four [[blinker](pages/blinker.md)]s.[^4]
