## Summary

Describes [[star-wars-rule](pages/star-wars-rule.md)]: the rule and its origin (Mirek Wójtowicz, March
1999), then its patterns.[^1]
- Still lifes of population 5, 8, 10, 12 and up; box-shaped still lifes form closed
  arenas.
- No period-2 or period-3 oscillators; "railroad" oscillators of every period from 4 up;
  common billiard tables.
- Mostly lightspeed "photon" spaceships, plus a c/6 diagonal one; guns at every period
  above 4.
- Photon circuitry, including logic gates, an adder and a Rule 110 emulator.
