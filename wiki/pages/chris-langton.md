# Chris G. Langton

## Description

Chris G. Langton wrote "Computation at the edge of chaos" (1990) in the Complex Systems
Group, Theoretical Division, Los Alamos National Laboratory.[^1] The paper summarizes his
University of Michigan Ph.D. thesis of the same title (1990). He first used λ in "Studying
artificial life with cellular automata" (*Physica D*, 1986). There he already suggested
that Wolfram's class IV sits between class II and class III (both cited via the 1990
paper, not read).[^2]

**Contributions in the wiki's sources.**

- The [[lambda-parameter](pages/lambda-parameter.md)], a one-number ordering of
  cellular-automaton rule space, and two ways to sample rules with it.[^3]
- The order-chaos phase transition in rule space, and the claim that the capacity for
  computation is greatest near it ([[edge-of-chaos](pages/edge-of-chaos.md)]).[^4]
- The "freezing problem" as an analogue of the halting problem.[^5]
- An upper bound added to von Neumann's
  [[complexity-threshold](pages/complexity-threshold.md)].[^6]
