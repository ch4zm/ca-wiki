# R-pentomino

## Description

The R-pentomino is a five-cell polyomino and the classic
[[methuselah](pages/methuselah.md)]. It takes 1,103 generations to stabilize, much
longer than any other pattern that fits in a 3 × 3 bounding box and any other polyomino
with 5 or fewer cells.[^1] It is the only one of the 12 pentominoes that does not end
quickly by vanishing, stabilizing or oscillating.[^2] Some 5-cell non-polyominoes last
slightly longer; its grandparents last 1,105 generations.[^3]

**What it produces.** Its evolution is one of the most important in Life:[^4]
- in generation 28, a B-heptaplet that evolves like the B-heptomino
  ([[twin-bees](pages/twin-bees.md)]);
- in generation 48, a Herschel;
- in generation 69, the first [[glider](pages/glider.md)] ever observed in Life,
  noticed by Richard K. Guy in 1970.

All of the common unstable objects (the T-tetromino, pre-honey farm, pi-heptomino,
[[queen-bee](pages/queen-bee.md)] and lumps of muck) appear during its evolution.[^5]
