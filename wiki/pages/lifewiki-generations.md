# LifeWiki: Generations

**Source:** raw/lifewiki-generations.md, saved from https://conwaylife.com/wiki/Generations (page last edited 27 August 2025).
**Date ingested:** 2026-09-25
**Type:** wiki article

## Summary

Defines [[generations-rule](pages/generations-rule.md)]s as the multistate generalization
of Life-like rules, in which cells that would die instead age through further states.[^1]
It gives the three-part state rule, the notation Bx/Sy/n and y/x/n (with /Cn and /Gn
variants), and the equivalence of any Life-like rule with its n = 2 Generations form.[^2]
It tables fifteen well-known examples, including [[brians-brain](pages/brians-brain.md)] and
[[star-wars-rule](pages/star-wars-rule.md)], and covers software support and extensions.[^3]
