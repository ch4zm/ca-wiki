## Summary

Extends [[isotropic-non-totalistic-rule](pages/isotropic-non-totalistic-rule.md)]s past
range 1.[^1]
- The "exploded" Moore neighbourhood pushes the four edge and four corner cells outward,
  so ordinary Hensel notation still works.
- Range-2 cross, knight and von Neumann neighbourhoods have their own letter systems,
  mostly supported only by CAViewer.
- A table of rulespaces counts distinct transitions: 6 for range-1 von Neumann, 13
  hexagonal, 51 Moore, 618 range-2 von Neumann, 2,105,872 range-2 Moore, about 3 × 10^21
  for 4D Moore. Most have no notation and no software.
- apgsearch can search range-2 von Neumann isotropic rules through a custom ruletable.
