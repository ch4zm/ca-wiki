## Summary

Describes [[larger-than-life](pages/larger-than-life.md)].[^1]
- A family of higher-range outer-totalistic rules from Kellie Michele Evans's 1996 thesis,
  simulated by Golly (from 3.0) and LifeViewer.
- MCell notation Rr,Cc,Mm,Ssmin..smax,Bbmin..bmax,Nn, now superseded by the HROT form.
- A table of named rules, from Life itself (R1,C2,S2-3,B3) to Bosco's Rule, Majority and
  Bugsmovie.
- Characteristic patterns: hollow "bugs", slow circular "solid ships", and "roomba bugs".
- A recipe for converting a rule to another range or neighbourhood by scaling its
  intervals.
