## Summary

Describes [[catagolue](pages/catagolue.md)], the online census fed by
[[apgsearch](pages/apgsearch.md)].[^1]
- **Scale.** Over 100 contributors; for asymmetric Life soups, about 5.9 × 10^14 soups and
  7.3 × 10^15 objects of 553,848 distinct types.
- **Results.** Long expandable lists of the still lifes, oscillators, spaceships and
  long-lived soups seen, for asymmetric soups, all symmetries, and slow-salvo data. The
  article keeps these per-object counts; this wiki does not reproduce them.
- **Other rules.** Over 20,000 rules investigated, from close Life variants to Day &
  Night and Bosco's Rule.
- **Verification.** Large censuses screen submitted "hauls" statistically and by peer
  review.
- **Beyond soups.** It accepts any rule name, symmetry and object code, so it doubles as a
  generic pattern inventory: slow-salvo products, search-program output piped in through
  stdin symmetries, and the cheapest known glider syntheses and glider guns.
- **Community.** User pages, discovery credits and badges.
